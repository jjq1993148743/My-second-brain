What is SKIMA

SKIMA is an illustration commission service created in Japan in 2015.

A Japanese artist has opened an overseas site with the hope of "spreading their wings around the world and connecting with people overseas."
We help you find Japanese artists and easily commission art.

SKIMA handles a wide range of genres, from Vtuber and PNGtuber illustrations, SNS icons, and wallpapers to logo designs and comic production.
Streamers and Vtubers who are doing this commercially, as well as hobbyists, are welcome to request this service.
You will meet a wide range of artists, from illustrators and graphic designers to riggers and animators.

How to get Commission artwork on SKIMA

  • Find a work of your choice in the category you wish to commission

  • Send a request from the works page

  • Communicate in the talk room once approval

  • Request the deliverey of the work, and the transaction will be complete.

How to get Pre-made Illustration on SKIMA

  • Browse the categories to find a work of art you'd like to purchase

  • Click the “Buy” button on the artwork’s page

  • Provide the necessary details and make your payment

  • Once payment is processed, you can download the artwork

FAQ

Can I sell my illustrations on this platform?

This site is for purchase only.

What payment methods are available?

We accept credit cards (VISA, MasterCard, AMERICAN EXPRESS), Apple Pay, and Google Pay for payment.

What is the purchase fee?

A flat 20% purchase fee is charged on the product price/commodity price.
The buyer is fully responsible for the purchase fee.

What is "CHEER!"?

"CHEER!" is a function that allows you to support the artists of the products you purchase.
You can gift any amount of money you like to the artist.
Along with CHEER!, you can also optionally send a comment of up to 200 characters.
Additionally, if you send CHEER!, you may receive a thank-you reaction from the artist.
*Please note: it's optional*CHEER! will incur a flat 22% handling fee, and the handling fee will be borne by the purchaser
