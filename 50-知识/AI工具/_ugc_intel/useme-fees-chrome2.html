Key terms

Signing up for Useme is free. We only charge a fee when you invoice and get paid. This lets us handle the legal side of every deal with your client, so you can work with clients worldwide without formal barriers.

You have two plans to choose from:

 

USEME FLEX

USEME COMFORT

COST

4.99%* of the invoice amount

(never less than $5 and never more than $69 per invoice)

subscription for $99 / month

PAYMENT METHOD

deducted from your client's payment

charged to your card

* This is the rate for invoices issued in USD, EUR, and GBP. For invoices in PLN, the fee is 9,8%.

Once you create an account, your default plan is Useme Flex. You can switch plans and move to a Useme Comfort subscription in the Plans tab.

Learn more about each plan benefits at useme.com/en/pricing.

Useme Flex

The service fee in the Useme Flex plan is 4.99% of the net invoice amount. You can check your payout after deducting the service fee in the calculator on your profile.

Fee range

The service fee is never less than $5 and never more than $69 per invoice. For example:

  • Invoice for $50 → you’ll pay the $5 fee instead of $2.5
  • Invoice for $1500 → you’ll pay the $69 fee instead of $74.85

How it’s charged

The fee is deducted automatically from your client's payment. When you settle a deal, your client pays into our account first. Then, we send the money to you, minus the service fee.

Useme Comfort

The Useme Comfort plan is a subscription for $99 per month. It renews automatically every month, but you can cancel it anytime.

If you invoice a client and they pay within your active subscription period, there will be no service fee.

Check how subscription payments work.

Other fees

Even with an active subscription, some services carry an extra cost:

  • Express bank transfer – 10 EUR/USD/GBP
  • Documents sent by mail – 20 EUR/USD/GBP
  • Currency exchange fees
  • Skipping the deposit request and deferred invoice payment: 19 EUR/USD/GBP

What's always free

You don't pay anything for:

  • Creating an account
  • Browsing jobs
  • Contacting employers and sending offers

Note for Polish tax residents

If your tax residence is in Poland, you can also access two more things:

  • Cashback for active users in Useme Club
  • Useme Plus subscription for $149 / 499 PLN
