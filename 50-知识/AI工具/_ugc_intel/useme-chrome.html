We make it possible for freelancers and businesses to work together across more than 170 countries – handling the invoicing, payments, and paperwork in between.

Freelancers choose Useme to work with clients worldwide without formal barriers. For businesses, it’s a safe and simple way to hire and pay international contractors. Both sides of the transaction enjoy convenient deal-making while we handle cross-border formalities and ensure compliance.

Freelancers and clients can use Useme to find new work opportunities and to settle payments for projects they've already completed.

As a freelancer, you can:

  • Settle deals with your current clients – you fill out an online form, we send a payment request to your client, they pay – done. We transfer the money to your chosen account in the currency of your choice and send an invoice to your client.
  • Find new jobs – apply for freelance projects on Useme Jobs and close the deal on one platform.

We'll send you the money once your client pays and accepts your work. Most payouts arrive within 24 hours, but in special cases, it may take up to a few business days.

Of course! Our Customer Service team oversees all the transactions processed on Useme, so you can count on them whenever you have a question or doubt. Just contact us. We reply on business days.

As a European company, we operate under strict GDPR regulations, ensuring strong data protection and compliance with international standards. We act as a bridge between you and your client from another country. This is how it works:

Suppose you have a client who ordered a graphic from you. We hire you based on our Terms and conditions and buy the graphic. Your job is done at this point. Next, we invoice your client for the graphic, so we resell your work to them. This way, we can handle all the formal aspects of the transaction and make it easy, fast, and safe for you.
