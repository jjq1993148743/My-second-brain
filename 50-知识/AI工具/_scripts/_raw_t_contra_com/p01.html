Hourfield is a 16-second 16:9 launch film for products that organise people's time: calendars, schedulers, booking and reservation apps, planners. Retype a few short lines, drop in your logo and rename the calendar's days and tiles (meetings, table bookings, classes, shifts): the week re-plans itself on screen and the film lands on your logo lockup. Our second version, Maréva, a fictional table-booking app, turns the working week into an evening of reservations on the same animation and timing. Built end-to-end through the SVGator MCP server. The calendar that re-plans its own week, the orbit that swirls into a single dot and the lines centred by their real letter shapes (fonts measured, not eyeballed) were computed in Python and written straight into a live SVGator project as 780 editable keyframes; I directed every note and finished it in the canvas. Maréva was made the same way, on the same timeline. Everything stays a real layer: 50 live text layers, named elements (headline, logo, product-name, tagline) and one logo image animated in two moments. Yes, there is a lot to edit: every line of the story, a whole week of calendar tiles, a palette in nine roles. A full rebrand takes longer than five minutes, and that is the honest trade: it shows how much a single SVGator file can carry, and how MCP makes a detailed, story-driven film practical to re-make, not just to watch. 📰 FRONT PAGE A launch-day ad that puts your product on the front page. Tuesday, October 14. Late City Edition. A blood orange soda came back. Shelves cleared in eleven minutes. Officials urged the public to remain calm. Friday morning, a phone outlived the news cycle. Its charger was reported missing, presumed retired. Saturday, 12:01 a.m. Cold brew hit the shelves. Nobody went home. None of it happened. All of it made the front page. 🗞️ Every launch wants to be news. Most of them get a banner. I couldn't find a single newspaper in SVGator's template gallery. So I built one. The Bellbird Times. The Kestrel Herald. The Nightjar Post. Three papers. One template. Zero redraws. Retype the headline, swap the photo and the logo. Every word is live text. Every layer has a name. The motion never changes. So what's your headline? The press never stops. ✨ 🛠️ Built in SVGator with the MCP. 1,038 named layers, 8,440 keyframes.
