FAQ

Is Buy Me a Coffee cheaper than Podia?

Buy Me a Coffee has no monthly fee. You pay a flat 5% only on what you actually earn, so if you make nothing one month, you pay nothing. Podia charges a monthly subscription before you've sold anything, and its entry plan still takes a 5% cut on top of that. You only reach 0% by paying for a higher plan. For most creators, especially early on, paying only when you earn just makes more sense.

Do I need any technical setup?

With Buy Me a Coffee, no. You pick a URL, add a short bio, connect Stripe, and you're live in a few minutes. Podia is a full course and website platform, so you're building out a site, your products, and email before you can really start selling.

Can supporters tip me, or does it have to be a product?

On Buy Me a Coffee, both work. People can leave a one-time tip, buy something from your shop, or join a membership. Podia is built around courses, digital products, and subscriptions, so there's no easy way for someone to just send a quick tip.

Do my supporters need an account?

Not on Buy Me a Coffee. They can support you in seconds with a card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay. Podia asks buyers to create an account to access what they bought, which is more friction for someone who just wants to show quick support.

Can I sell digital products too?

Yes. Buy Me a Coffee has a free shop built in for downloads, templates, presets, and PDFs, right alongside your tips and memberships. Podia handles digital products well too, but everything sits behind a paid monthly plan.

What about my email list?

With Buy Me a Coffee you can email your supporters directly, with no subscriber cap and no extra fee. Podia includes email free only up to 100 subscribers, and the cost climbs as your list grows.

What if I already have an audience somewhere else?

That's the best case. Drop your Buy Me a Coffee link in your newsletter, video descriptions, or social bios, and your audience can support you right away. No migration, no rebuilding anything. With Podia, you'd be setting up a full site and product catalog first.

How quickly can I get started?

A few minutes on Buy Me a Coffee. No hosting, no theme setup, no monthly commitment. Pick a URL, write a bio, connect Stripe, and you can start receiving support today. Getting a Podia site and products ready to sell takes a lot longer.
