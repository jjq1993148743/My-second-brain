Summary

Position: MLOps ( Python Cor, ML engineer)

Location: USA any, or Ideally San Jose, CA; San Diego, CA; Wilton, CT

Duration: 12+Months

Must Have: Python, Azure, ML, AKS, PyTorch

Here is JD below:

  • Hands-on experience developing, fine-tuning, and deploying ML and foundation models in production.
  • Strong Python skills and expertise with PyTorch and/or TensorFlow.
  • Experience building and operating MLOps pipelines, including CI/CD, model lifecycle management, and monitoring.
  • Experience deploying ML workloads on Azure and Kubernetes (AKS).
  • Strong software engineering practices, including testing, code quality, and automation.
  • Ability to work effectively with ML researchers, data scientists, and cross-functional engineering teams.
  • Experience managing multi-GPU-based workloads and optimizing model performance.

Nice to Have:

  • Experience with distributed training frameworks and large-scale model training.
  • Familiarity with ML platform engineering, including training infrastructure and workload orchestration.
  • Exposure to platform operations, reliability, governance, and compliance requirements.
  • Experience mentoring engineers or leading technical initiatives.
