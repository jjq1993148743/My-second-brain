    Frequently Asked Questions

    • How do I earn money on Facer?
      You earn revenue when users sync your paid watch faces through Facer Premium, or when they purchase them individually. The more users choose your designs, the more you earn.

    • How and when do I get paid?
      Earnings are calculated monthly and distributed through supported payout options. A full performance and revenue breakdown is available in your dashboard.

    • What kind of support do partners receive?
      Partners get access to dedicated support for program-related questions, tips on publishing best practices, and ongoing updates from the Facer team to help them succeed.

    • What kinds of designs can I sell?
      Analog, digital, hybrid, interactive, or animated—any style is welcome, as long as it's original and high-quality. Infringement on copyrights and trademarks is not tolerated.

    • How much can I make as a Facer Creator Partner?
      Your earnings depend on how many users sync or purchase your designs. Some partners in the Facer Creator Partner Program make a living from their watch face creations.

    • Do I retain ownership of my work?
      Yes, your designs remain yours. By publishing on Facer, you grant permission for your work to be distributed and promoted through the platform.
