How do I cancel Facer Creator Pro?
https://help.facer.io/hc/en-us/articles/360018780293-How-do-I-cancel-Facer-Creator-Pro

You can cancel your subscription any time. No hard feelings :) This will revert your account to a regular Facer Creator account after your current subscription period ends. After this, you will no longer have access to any Creator Pro features. Here’s how to cancel your subscription: On your web browser, go to www.facer.io . Login to your Facer account (the same one used to purchase Facer Creator Pro). Navigate to your account management page by clicking the "Manage Account" link in the header user dropdown (or you can get there via this llnk: www.facer.io/user/me/account ). On the account management page, click the 'pencil' icon next to where it says 'Subscribed' in the Facer Creator Pro section under the "Products" label. Tap Cancel . After successful cancelation, your subscription will lapse at the end of the current period and will not be renewed.
