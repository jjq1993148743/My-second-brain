About JobBoard.io

JobBoard.io is job board software platform, not a recruitment website. We host the software to run job boards – we do not offer or list employment opportunities.

Looking to launch your own niche job board? With JobBoard.io you can start your own job board in just 30 seconds, with no installation, coding or servers required – we handle all the technical elements so you can focus on growing your job board.
