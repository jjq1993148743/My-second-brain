FAQ

How many templates can I submit?+

Once you have submitted at least one template to Prototion, you become a Maker. Makers can add as many templates as they want. After a review of the template, it is published onto the platform and you receive an email informing you about it.

How do Makers get paid?+

Makers receive a pay-out at the end of every month. You can use Stripe or Paypal for the same. Soon, we are planning to launch a feature where the maker can directly withdraw their earnings from their dashboard.

Do you charge a commission?+

Bringing more users on board (so that our Makers can sell more templates), maintaining the platform, and enabling online payments via service providers, comes with a cost. In order to make up for this, we do charge a commission. This is approximately 20% of the template's selling price and is automatically deducted when your template is sold to any user.

How can I sell more templates on Prototion?+

Adding descriptive images, writing a long description (at least 400 words), and pointing out every single feature of your template, are by far the most impactful things you can do to get more sales. The images help customers fully understand what you're offering while the long description ensures your template shows up in Google's search results, ultimately getting you more sales.
