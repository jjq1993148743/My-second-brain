How it works

How does a Hatchwise design contest actually work?

You write one brief and many professional creatives design against it. You compare real concepts instead of portfolios, choose the work you want and own it outright. Three steps, no recruiting, no retainer.

  1. Step 01

    Write the brief and set the budget

    Describe the work you need, the audience it speaks to and the files you expect. Pick the contest budget, which is the prize the winning creative is paid. Logo contests start at $99, all inclusive.

  2. Step 02

    Review original concepts as they arrive

    Creatives from around the world submit custom work made for your brief. You rate entries, ask for changes and steer the direction while the contest is live. You can award a winner as soon as you see work you like, on any contest. The ten day window is the guaranteed time for entries to keep arriving, not a wait before you can award.

  3. Step 03

    Award the work and take full rights

    Choose the concept you want. Full commercial rights and the production files transfer to you, so you can trademark, print and publish it without any further licence.

Buying questions

Graphic design service questions, answered

What is Hatchwise?

Hatchwise is a graphic design service that connects businesses with a global community of professional designers and namers. Instead of hiring one designer, you post your brief and receive original custom concepts from many creatives, then choose the one you want to own. Hatchwise has been operating since 2008 and is rated 4.8 out of 5 on Trustpilot.

What kind of graphic design work can Hatchwise do?

Logos and brand identity, website and landing page design, t-shirt and apparel graphics, business cards and stationery, brochures, banner ads, packaging, icons, illustration and custom graphic design, plus naming work such as company names, product names, domain names, slogans and taglines.

How much does a graphic design service cost at Hatchwise?

Logo design contests start at $99 all inclusive. Every package price already includes a $49 listing fee, and the rest of the price is the prize paid to the winning creative. Full pricing for every category is published on the pricing page, and Hatchwise Unlimited is a flat monthly rate with no contracts.

How long does a contest take?

A standard design contest has a 10 day submission window, during which creatives submit work and you request revisions. The 48 Hour Rush upgrade returns results in 48 hours. Once a contest ends you can extend it for a small fee per extra day, and extensions are free on Gold and Custom tiers and on Hatchwise Unlimited. Hatchwise Unlimited is a subscription, so you run projects back to back for a flat monthly rate.

Can I award a winner before the 10 days are up?

Yes. You can award a winner at any time while a contest is active, including before the 10 day window ends, and awarding simply ends the contest. The 10 days is the guaranteed window for entries to arrive, not a wait before you can award. Separately, a refund under the money back guarantee requires a contest to have run its full length with no winner awarded.

Is Hatchwise a good alternative to hiring an in house designer or an agency?

It is a good fit when you need professional design work without a full time salary or an agency retainer, and when seeing several directions is more useful than one designer's single interpretation. An agency is still the better choice when you need deep strategic work, ongoing brand governance or someone embedded in your team day to day.

How does Hatchwise compare with a freelancer?

With a freelancer you buy one person's interpretation of your brief and manage the relationship yourself. With Hatchwise you see many interpretations from different creatives for a fixed price, and only pay the published contest price. Freelancers are usually the better route for long running work that depends on knowing your product intimately.

Do I own the design I choose?

Yes. Full rights to the winning work transfer to you when the contest is awarded, so you can use it commercially, trademark it and adapt it.

What if none of the concepts are right?

Hatchwise has a money back guarantee. If you do not receive at least the number of entries that come with the package you purchased, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your contest submission period ending by contacting Hatchwise support. The contest must run its full length first, 10 days or 48 hours for rush contests, and contests where a winner has already been awarded are not eligible. Full terms are on the guarantee page.

How do designers get paid?

Creatives are paid the prize when their entry is awarded, with payouts issued on the 1st and the 14th of each month via PayPal.

Is there a subscription option for ongoing graphic design?

Yes. Hatchwise Unlimited is a flat monthly plan starting at $449 for teams that need design work continuously, with no contracts and cancellation at any time. It includes a dedicated project manager, free upgrades and extensions and phone support, you run projects back to back for the whole subscription, and first time subscribers can request a full refund of the subscription fee in writing within 14 days of their first contest being started.
