How to redeem a Facer Premium coupon via the Facer website
https://help.facer.io/hc/en-us/articles/12409938951579-How-to-redeem-a-Facer-Premium-coupon-via-the-Facer-website

If you received a coupon code for Facer Premium on Facer.io, redeeming it is easy! Just follow the quick steps below: 1. Open your browser and paste the provided link: https://www.facer.io/premium 2. Click on "I have a coupon!" 3. Enter the provided coupon in the text field, then hit "Apply". T he promotional period will be mentioned under the Subscribe button 4. Click on the blue "Subscribe" button 5. Use the registration form to create a new FACER account on the next step, then click on Sign up (if you already have a FACER account just switch to the sign-in form by clicking on the "sign in" link at the bottom) 6. Add your Credit Card info on the next page, then click on "Subscribe" (this is required for successful activation; if you have been offered a free trial, you will not get billed until the trial is over) 7. That's it! Your subscription is now active! You can confirm by checking that you have a gold star next to your profile icon as shown in the screenshot below. 8. With Facer Premium activated, you can now sync any of the 400k watch faces available on Facer. You can access your account from our iPhone or Android app as well (just make sure to login in the apps with the same credentials you used to activate your account).
