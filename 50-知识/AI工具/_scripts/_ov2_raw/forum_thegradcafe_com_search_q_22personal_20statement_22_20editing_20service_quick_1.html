            7. What are some effective ways to prepare for PSLE?

              iamharry posted a topic in Coursework, Advising, and Exams

              PSLE preparation becomes much easier when students combine concept revision with regular exam-style practice. Instead of only reading notes, it can be useful to attempt timed questions, review mistakes, and identify topics that need more attention. For Maths, practising problem sums and short-answer questions can help students become more comfortable with different question formats.
